## Worst-case byte arithmetic for a FULL 1000-id chunk
Decompose the 144-byte real payload:
  - envelope + tenant block (everything except the ids):
      {"asset_ids":[]  ,"platform_tenant":{"ID":1,"Name":"Onyx Security",
      "SchemaName":"onyx_security","UUID":"61750f27-8fbd-42a4-80cf-4191cfb2371a"}}
    = 144 - len("230") = 144 - 3 = 141 bytes fixed overhead for THIS tenant.
    (Tenant name/schema are bounded: a schema name is <=63 chars; even a maximal
     tenant block stays a small fixed constant, ~250 bytes.)
  - each additional id adds len(str(id)) + 1 (the comma). asset ids are bigint (int64)
    serial keys.

Bounds for the capped 1000-id chunk (the maximum a single workflow input can carry):
  - realistic prod ids (8 digits): 1000 * (8 + 1) - 1 = 8,999 bytes of ids
      total ~ 141 + 8,999 = ~9.1 KB
  - theoretical int64 max (19 digits, 9223372036854775807): 1000 * (19 + 1) - 1 = 19,999
      total ~ 141 + 19,999 = ~20 KB

## Bound vs the Temporal payload cap
Temporal's single-payload gRPC limit is 2 MB (2,097,152 bytes). The worst-case FULL
1000-id chunk is ~20 KB even with maximal int64 ids and a maximal tenant block — two
orders of magnitude under the cap. The 1000-id chunk cap (MAX_ASSET_IDS_PER_RECALC_WORKFLOW)
is what keeps this bounded: an app_definition binding more than 1000 assets is split
into multiple <=1000-id workflow inputs, so no single IssueDetectionWorkflow input can
ever approach the 2 MB boundary regardless of tenant size.
