## Conclusion
- Top fan-out in this dev tenant: 124 (NULL/unbound group) overall; 2 for the largest REAL
  app_definition_id ('mcp-remote').
- Neither exceeds the 1000-id chunk cap. The cap bounds each Temporal workflow input regardless
  of tenant size: an app_definition fanning out to >1000 assets is split into multiple <=1000-id
  workflow inputs. In this dev tenant no app_definition approaches that bound, so exactly one
  workflow is started per real app_definition. The chunking remains the correct guard for large
  production tenants where a single app_definition can bind far more version/endpoint rows.
