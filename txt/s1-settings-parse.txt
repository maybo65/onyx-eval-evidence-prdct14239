WHY THIS IS THE SAFE SIDE (honest inventory):
  Emission is gated on POSITIVE confirmation of a live, valid install, never on
  the mere presence of a config tree. Per the detector's own rationale, the
  'codearts' binary lives INSIDE ~/.codeartsdoer/installers/, so an uninstalled
  CLI leaves the whole config home (and a settings.json) on disk. Defaulting an
  unreadable/garbage/empty-security config to NOT-detected prevents the scanner
  from minting a false or duplicate CodeArts asset from leftover residue —
  over-claiming an agent would create phantom inventory and false security
  posture. Fail-closed here means: do not claim what you cannot validate.
